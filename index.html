01 Problem statement

Connecting museum information and services.

When heritage information, educational materials, immersive experiences, and visitor services are spread across separate channels, visitors can find it difficult to connect discovery with learning. Museum staff also face additional work maintaining those channels.

General objective

To design, develop, test, and evaluate a WebXR-enabled digital heritage platform that supports accessible, interactive, and immersive heritage learning for onsite and offsite users.

Specific objectives

  1. Design an integrated platform

    Present curated heritage information, bilingual content, inclusive access, and selected immersive experiences.

  2. Implement the required modules

    Develop heritage and educational content, immersive features, accessibility controls, visitor services, and administration.

  3. Test the system’s functions

    Check the major public and administrative features against their requirements using documented black-box test cases.

  4. Evaluate user acceptance

    Assess usefulness, ease of use, immersive experience, accessibility, and related user-evaluation criteria.

02 Methodology

Research and development process.

The study used a descriptive, developmental, and evaluative research design. Interviews, onsite observations, document review, and museum-personnel consultations informed requirements. Development followed an iterative Agile model, with black-box functional testing and questionnaire-based user evaluation.

  1. Planning

    Identify museum needs, intended users, available content, and project requirements.

  2. Design

    Plan the interface, navigation, database, workflows, and immersive interactions.

  3. Development

    Build and integrate the public modules, visitor services, and administrative tools.

  4. Testing

    Check specified functions, address issues, and retest affected components.

  5. Deployment

    Make the working platform available for intended use and evaluation.

  6. Feedback & refinement

    Review observations and evaluation findings to guide revisions.

03 System features & screenshots

Inside the platform.

  1. Digital heritage & education

    Museum information, digital exhibits, online gallery collections, and educational resources.

  2. Interactive experiences

    A ten-stop Heritage Trail, 360° virtual tour, site map, selected AR content, and 3D/WebXR environments.

  3. Accessibility support

    English and Filipino content, text-size controls, high contrast, narration, subtitles, and responsive layouts.

  4. Visitor services & participation

    Accounts, bookmarks, visit requests, feedback, and moderated community or educator contributions.

  5. Administrative management

    Content and user management, submission review, booking coordination, and visitor-feedback review.
