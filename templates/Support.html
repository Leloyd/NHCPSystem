Frequently Asked Questions

How do I access the virtual/WebXR experience?

Go to the Interactive module and choose either the WebXR experience or fallback mode if your device/browser does not support WebXR.

Can I use this platform on a mobile phone?

Yes. The platform is mobile-responsive and designed for low- to mid-range devices, with non-XR alternatives when needed.

How can teachers use this in class?

Teachers can use digital exhibits, guided trails, and quizzes for classroom discussions, blended learning, and assignments.

How do I submit a story or feedback?

Visit E-Participation and use the Story Submission or Feedback form. You will receive a confirmation after submission.
