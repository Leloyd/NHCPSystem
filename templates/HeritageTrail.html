Journey overview

One path, one story, one clear learning flow

The trail always follows the same order so visitors build understanding gradually instead of jumping between features. Digital tools are there to support each stop, not replace the journey.

Learning focus

What helps a visitor understand the site from the very beginning?

Start with orientation, rules, and museum context so the visitor is ready to understand the church and underground spaces more deeply.

Follow-up activities

Activity checkpoints for the learning path

These checkpoints can be used in person or online to help visitors process what they learned before entering the church, after exploring the museum, and at the end of the full heritage journey.

Mid-trail activity

Museum to Church Learning Check

Pause after the museum section and ask visitors to connect the visitor rules, museum artifacts, and cemetery history before moving toward the church interpretation stops.

End-of-trail activity

Final Assessment & Exit Reflection

End the journey by asking learners to match each major stop to what it taught them about orientation, architecture, materials, ritual use, and historical memory.
