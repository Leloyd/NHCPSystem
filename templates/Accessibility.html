Inclusive Accessibility

Making heritage accessible to everyone through inclusive design and assistive technologies.

Accessibility Features

Visual Accessibility

  • • High contrast mode
  • • Adjustable text sizes
  • • Screen reader compatibility
  • • Alt text for all images
  • • Audio descriptions

Hearing Accessibility

  • • Closed captions for videos
  • • Written transcripts
  • • Visual alerts and notifications
  • • Sign language interpretation
  • • Text-based alternatives

Multilingual Support

  • • English and Filipino content
  • • Easy-to-read formats
  • • Translation tools
  • • Cultural context notes
  • • Simplified language options

Physical Accessibility

  • • Wheelchair-accessible routes
  • • Ramps and elevators
  • • Accessible restrooms
  • • Reserved parking spaces
  • • Seating areas throughout

Cognitive Accessibility

  • • Clear navigation
  • • Consistent layouts
  • • Simplified information
  • • Visual guides and maps
  • • Predictable interactions

Assistive Technologies

  • • Audio guides available
  • • Touch-screen interfaces
  • • Voice navigation
  • • Braille signage
  • • Tactile models

Virtual Experience Accessibility

Experience from Anywhere

Our virtual platform ensures that everyone can explore the Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery, regardless of physical location or mobility constraints.

  • ✓360° virtual tours with keyboard and screen reader navigation
  • ✓Detailed audio descriptions for all visual elements
  • ✓Downloadable transcripts and resources
  • ✓Adjustable playback speeds and pause options

Need Assistance?

Our team is here to help ensure you have the best possible experience. Contact us for personalized accessibility support.

Our Commitment

We are committed to meeting WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards and continually improving accessibility for all visitors. Our heritage should be accessible to everyone.

Have suggestions for improving accessibility? We'd love to hear from you.

About the Museum

Preserving the heritage and history of the Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery, a unique testament to Filipino ingenuity and cultural preservation.
