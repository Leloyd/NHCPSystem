Interactive & Immersive Experiences

Step into history with cutting-edge interactive experiences that bring the past to life.

Immersive Experiences

Augmented Reality Tour

Use your smartphone to see historical overlays and virtual reconstructions of the cemetery as it appeared in different time periods.

360° Virtual Reality

Explore the underground crypts in stunning 360° VR. Compatible with VR headsets or view on your browser.

Interactive Map

Navigate through the cemetery with our detailed interactive map, complete with historical points of interest and stories.

Audio Walking Tour

Listen to expert narration as you explore, with stories about the architecture, history, and people connected to this site.

Educational Games

Learn through play with interactive quizzes, puzzles, and games designed for all ages.

Time Travel Experience

Witness key historical moments through immersive multimedia experiences and reconstructions.

Featured: AR Time Portal

Point your camera at historical markers to unlock AR experiences that show the cemetery during different eras - from construction in 1845 to its role in the Philippine Revolution.

Download our mobile app to unlock the full AR experience

Educational Games & Activities

Architecture Puzzle

Reconstruct the underground cemetery's floor plan while learning about Spanish colonial architecture.

History Quest

Follow clues and answer questions about the cemetery's role in Philippine history to unlock achievements.

Memory Match

Match historical photos, artifacts, and facts to learn about the cemetery's rich heritage.

Timeline Builder

Create an interactive timeline of significant events in the cemetery's history and share with friends.

About the Museum

Preserving the heritage and history of the Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery, a unique testament to Filipino ingenuity and cultural preservation.
