Calibration Guide

For precise hotspot placement
  1. Upload your aerial image to /static/images/site-map/aerial-view.jpg.
  2. Open /site-map?calibrate=1 and click points directly on the image.
  3. Copy the shown x,y values into /static/data/site-map-hotspots.json.
  4. Refresh /site-map to review marker alignment and adjust as needed.
