Heritage Overview · I Pangkalahatang-tanaw · I

The Nagcarlan
Underground Cemetery Ang Libingan sa Ilalim
ng Lupa ng Nagcarlan

THE NAGCARLAN Underground Cemetery, built in 1845 under Fray Vicente Velloc, stands as a historic landmark that served as a burial site, a revolutionary meeting place, and a hideout of guerillas. Declared a National Historical Landmark on 24 October 1981, it continues to embody Nagcarlan's faith, history, and collective memory.

ANG LIBINGAN sa Ilalim ng Lupa ng Nagcarlan, itinayo sa patnubay ni Padre Vicente Velloc nong 1845, ay isang makasaysayang palatandaan na nagsilbing libingan, tagpuan ng mga rebolusyonaryo, at taguan ng mga gerilya noong panahon ng digmaan. Idineklarang Pambansang Palatandaang Pangkasaysayan noong 24 Oktubre 1981, patuloy nitong kinakatawan ang pananampalataya, kasaysayan, at kabuuang gunita ng Nagcarlan.

Heritage Overview · II Pangkalahatang-tanaw · II

Inscriptions of
Faith Mga Inskripsiyon ng
Pananampalataya

FILIPINO GRAVESTONES often bear Catholic symbols such as RI.P. (Requiescat in pace), D.E.P. (Descansar en paz), and D.O.M. (Deo Optimo Maximo), signifying prayers for the dead. Among ordinary Filipinos, the local inscription S.L.N., meaning "Sumalangit Nawa" (May their soul ascend to heaven) is more common.

SA MGA PUNTOD sa Pilipinas, makikita ang mga panrelihiyong sagisag tulad ng RI.P. (Requiescas in pace), D.E.P. (Descansar en paz), at D.O.M. (Deo Optimo Maximo). Ipinapahiwatig ng mga ito ang panalangin at pag-aalay ng kaluluwa sa Diyos. Sa mga puntod ng karaniwang mamamayan, madalas namang nakasulat ang S.L.N. o Sumalangit Nawa.

Heritage Overview · III Pangkalahatang-tanaw · III

Shelter of
Guerrillas Kublihan ng mga
Gerilya

THE NAGCARLAN Cemetery became a stronghold for Filipino guerrillas during the Japanese occupation. Colonel Guillermo "Billy" Igay Aynaga Sr., founder of the First Laguna Division of the Fil-American Irregular Troops, led the movement until his capture and death in 1944. His remains were later moved to the Libingan ng mga Bayani.

NAGING KUBLIHAN at kuta ng mga gerilyang Pilipino ang Libingan ng Nagcarlan noong pananakop ng mga Hapon. Pinamunuan ito ni Koronel Guillermo "Billy" Igay Aynaga Sr., na nagtatag ng Unang Sangay ng Fil-American Irregular Troops sa Laguna. Siya ay nadakip at pinaslang noong 1944, at kalaunan ay inilibing sa Libingan ng mga Bayani.

Responsible Visiting

Museum
Rules

Observe Silence

Observe silence, be mindful of other guests, and do not loiter inside the museum.

Dress Respectfully

Hats and caps must be removed as a sign of respect to the museum and historical landmark.

Photography Permission

No flash photography. Video and photo shoots are allowed only with permission from the museum management.

Protect Displays

Do not touch displays and artifacts.

No Food or Drinks

Do not eat or drink inside the museum and chapel.

Logbook Registration

Visitors are required to sign the guest book or logbook upon arrival. For larger groups, the coordinator will register the group on their behalf.

Follow Guide Instructions

Always follow your museum guide's instructions. In case of emergency, stay calm and follow your museum guide's safety instructions.

Animals Policy

Pets and other animals are not allowed inside the museum. However, animals required by individuals with physical or mental health disabilities to assist with mobility or stability are permitted if they are properly controlled and restrained.

Dispose Waste Properly

All waste must be properly disposed of in designated trash cans or bins to help maintain the cleanliness and integrity of the site.

Visitor Limit Control

Museum personnel have the authority to limit the number of visitors inside the museum to prevent overcrowding and disruptive behavior.

Right to Deny Entry

The Curator or Officer-in-Charge has the authority to deny entry or remove visitors who are unruly, noisy, intoxicated, or who refuse to comply with the museum's rules and regulations.
