About the Site

The Nagcarlan Underground Cemetery is a rare architectural gem built during the Spanish colonial period in 1845. Constructed under the supervision of Franciscan friars, this unique structure served as both a cemetery and a chapel, featuring an underground crypt system that was revolutionary for its time.

The cemetery's brick vaults and arched passages demonstrate the sophisticated engineering and architectural knowledge of the period. Its design reflects a blend of European cemetery traditions adapted to the tropical Philippine climate.

During the Philippine Revolution, the underground chambers served as a secret meeting place for the Katipuneros, adding another layer of historical significance to this remarkable structure.

Cemetery History

1845

Construction Era

Built under the direction of Franciscan friars as a modern burial ground featuring underground crypts. The structure represented a significant advancement in cemetery architecture in the Philippines.

1896

Revolutionary Period

The underground chambers became a secret meeting place for members of the Katipunan during the Philippine Revolution against Spanish colonial rule.

1973

National Landmark

Declared a National Historical Landmark by the National Historical Institute, recognizing its unique architectural and historical significance.

Today

Museum & Heritage Site

Now serves as a museum and cultural heritage site, welcoming visitors from around the world to experience this unique piece of Philippine history.

House Rules

Respect the Heritage

This is a sacred historical site. Please maintain a respectful demeanor and speak in moderate tones.

Photography Guidelines

Photography is allowed without flash. Please do not touch or lean on historical artifacts and structures.

Group Tours

Groups of 10 or more must arrange guided tours in advance. Follow your guide's instructions at all times.

Preservation

Do not touch, write on, or damage any part of the structure. Help us preserve this heritage site for future generations.

Safety First

Watch your step, especially in the underground areas. Children must be accompanied by adults at all times.
