Digital Exhibits

AR Content Preview

Marker-based overlays that connect physical site points to short cultural context, archival visuals, and teaching prompts.

AR Marker Demo Frame

Live camera + overlay will be connected in next phase

How It Works

  1. 1. Open this page on your mobile device.
  2. 2. Point your camera at a supported site marker.
  3. 3. Tap overlays to read context and artifact notes.
  4. 4. Save your reflections for class discussion.

Device Checklist

  • • Android Chrome / iOS Safari (latest)
  • • Camera permission enabled
  • • Good lighting on marker surface
  • • Stable internet for overlay assets

Featured Marker Experiences

Marker A1

Chapel Facade Details

Shows reconstruction overlay of facade elements and key architectural annotations.

Teaching prompt: What details remained unchanged after restoration?

Marker B2

Crypt Corridor Layers

Compares present-day corridor views with historical references and airflow notes.

Teaching prompt: How does design affect ritual movement?

Marker C3

Historic Marker Text

Highlights inscription terms with glossary support and bilingual label placeholders.

Teaching prompt: Why do word choices matter in public history?

Implementation Note

This page is the UI scaffold for marker-based AR. In the next phase, connect WebAR libraries and marker assets to activate camera scanning and live overlays.
